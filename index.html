Questions

Good to know.

Does SortGlass delete my photos?

Only when you confirm. Swiping up adds a photo to SortGlass Trash, which is just a queue. When you choose Delete in Trash, iOS asks you to confirm, and confirmed items move to Recently Deleted in Apple Photos, where Apple generally keeps them for up to 30 days unless you delete them sooner.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. SortGlass works directly on your Apple Photos library, so favorites, albums, ratings and deletions sync through iCloud Photos according to your settings. Photos stored only in iCloud may download when you open them.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Star ratings require iOS 27 or later and SortGlass Pro. The Liquid Glass design appears on iOS 26 and later.

Is there an iPad or Mac version?

Not yet. SortGlass is available for iPhone today. iPad support and Mac support for Apple silicon Macs are coming in version 1.1.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore Pro on another device, open More → SortGlass Pro → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
