Website accessibility

We want people with disabilities to be able to learn about SortGlass and contact support. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as an improvement target, not a claim that every requirement has been independently certified.

Using the website

  • Use Tab and Shift–Tab to move between links and controls. On narrow screens, the Menu button opens the site navigation, and Escape closes it.
  • Use the pause controls to stop the homepage demos. With Reduce Motion enabled, the demos stay still until you interact with them.
  • The guide explains the actions shown in its video and screenshots, so you can follow it without watching them.
  • Browser zoom and narrow layouts are supported.

Report a barrier or ask for help

Email support@sortglass.com if something prevents you from using the site or if you need information in another format. If comfortable, tell us which page or control caused trouble and what browser or assistive technology you use. You do not need to disclose a disability, medical information, or private photos.

Accessibility needs ongoing testing, including with assistive technology. Third-party destinations such as the App Store are operated by their respective providers. Reporting an issue helps us identify and prioritize fixes; this page is not a guarantee of legal compliance.
