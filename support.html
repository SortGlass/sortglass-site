Common questions

Does SortGlass delete my photos?

Only when you confirm. Swiping up adds a photo to SortGlass Trash, which is just a queue. When you choose Delete in Trash, iOS asks you to confirm, and confirmed items move to Recently Deleted in Apple Photos, where Apple generally keeps them for up to 30 days unless you delete them sooner.

How do I get back a photo I deleted?

Open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, select the photo and choose Recover. Items stay there for a limited time, generally up to 30 days, unless they were deleted from there sooner.

SortGlass can’t see some of my photos.

Check Photos access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SortGlass. With limited access, SortGlass can only show the photos you selected. Photos stored only in iCloud may need to download when you open them.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. SortGlass works directly on your Apple Photos library, so favorites, albums, ratings and deletions sync through iCloud Photos according to your settings.

What does the free version include?

Up to 100 Trash and Album actions each week, with the count starting again every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Favorites, Browse, and Undo are always included. SortGlass Pro unlocks unlimited organizing, ratings, combined and saved filters, and every theme and icon.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open More → SortGlass Pro → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, then choose SortGlass Pro. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds for App Store purchases. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I redeem an offer code?

In the App Store app, tap your account picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Star ratings require iOS 27 or later and SortGlass Pro. The Liquid Glass design appears on iOS 26 and later.
