Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

SortGlass is operated by Cyrus Press, an individual developer based in California, United States. This policy describes information handled when you use the SortGlass app on iPhone, iPad or Mac, including public App Store releases and TestFlight builds, visit sortglass.com, or contact support. For information for which the operator determines the purposes and means of processing, Cyrus Press is the data controller.

Privacy questions and requests: support@sortglass.com.

1. Overview

SortGlass helps you review and organize your Apple Photos library. The app processes the media you authorize on your device. SortGlass does not operate a server that receives your photo or video library, sell your personal information, or use your library for advertising or AI training. Recognizing what's in your photos and searching them happen on your device.

Some features described here, such as Library Scans, the Vault and App Lock, arrive in versions of SortGlass after 1.1. This policy covers each feature where your version includes it.

The app has no advertising SDK, advertising-identifier integration, or separate developer-operated behavioral analytics service. This does not mean that no information ever leaves your device: Apple services, TestFlight, website hosting, support communications, and sharing you initiate involve the information described below.

2. Photos and videos

With your permission, SortGlass accesses authorized photos and videos to display, organize, rate, favorite, add items to albums, hide, duplicate, export, edit captions and keywords, adjust dates and places, and request deletion. Associated information can include library identifiers, capture dates, media types, dimensions, duration, album membership, favorites, ratings, captions and keywords, available file sizes, and embedded metadata such as recorded photo locations.

Edits you make through SortGlass can change your Apple Photos library. Apple may synchronize these changes through iCloud Photos according to your settings. Apple Photos may download cloud-stored originals when needed; that is different from SortGlass uploading your library to its own server.

The Vault keeps the photos and videos you move into it encrypted on your device. Moving an item into the Vault encrypts it, checks the encrypted copy, and then asks Apple Photos to delete the original, which Apple Photos keeps in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Items you share to SortGlass from another app are encrypted in the share sheet, and SortGlass may later offer to delete their originals from Apple Photos. Items leave the Vault only when you export them.

You control Photos access in your device's Settings (System Settings on a Mac). Restricting or withdrawing access can limit app features.

3. Local preferences and organizing information

SortGlass stores information needed for its features locally, including appearance preferences, organizing progress, reviewed-item identifiers, cleanup history, statistics, goals, achievements, and a processed-item allowance used for free access limits. This information supports your experience; it is not a developer dashboard of your photo activity.

Clearing dashboard statistics does not reset the free allowance, delete your photos, or undo changes already made to Apple Photos.

Photo search uses a local metadata index containing accessible asset references, filenames, file types, file sizes, capture dates, dimensions, and available camera, lens and focal-length details. Normal app updates retain this index; deleting the app normally removes it.

Library Scans reads your photos on your device so you can search them and find clutter. Apple's Vision recognizes objects, scenes and words in photos, a search model built into SortGlass (SigLIP) turns each photo into numbers that match descriptions you type, SortGlass notes each photo's main colors, and Vision compares photos to find copies and look-alikes. The results are kept in SortGlass's storage on your device, out of device backups, and contain no copies of your photos. When a photo leaves your library, for example because you deleted it or moved it to the Vault, what was read in it is removed. Delete Objects & Text Data, in Privacy & Photos Access, removes what Objects & Text found.

Library Scans reads photos stored on your device. Photos stored only in iCloud are downloaded to be read only if you allow it with Download iCloud Photos; Apple Photos may then download them, or full originals, even with Optimize iPhone Storage on. Apple controls the resulting Photos cache and its storage use.

To show the On This Day widget, SortGlass saves a copy of one photo from that day, together with the counts its widgets show, in a folder on your device that only SortGlass and its own extensions can read.

4. iCloud Sync and the Vault's iCloud copy

iCloud Sync (More → iCloud Sync) keeps SortGlass's settings, backups and scan results in your private iCloud database, so your devices signed in to the same Apple Account share them and a reinstall can pick them up. It is not a developer analytics feed, it does not upload copies of your photos or videos, and SortGlass has no server of its own.

In SortGlass 1.1, each of these options is off until you turn it on. In later versions, a new install starts with every category on, an install already in use keeps its choices, and Library Scans is on unless you turn it off. One switch turns iCloud Sync on or off, and each category has its own switch on each device:

  • Appearance & Settings: themes, colors, saved themes and colors, launch and glass options, the starting tab and similar preferences, and the area of a home you set for Trips. Photos per row and grid style stay on each device.
  • Pinned Albums & Order: the names, Apple Photos' cross-device identifiers and order of pinned albums and folders, and the Albums tab's order.
  • Goals, History & Statistics: goal settings, achievements and when they were earned, and recent Trash and album actions: Apple Photos' cross-device identifier for each photo involved, the album name, the date and whether the action came from an iPhone, iPad or Mac. Statistics can include lifetime statistics, action history, photo identifiers used to avoid double-counting, dates, known file sizes and library totals.
  • Saved Filters: filter names and selections, including categories, ratings, file types, search terms, album references and On This Day dates, plus removal records so older devices do not restore deleted saved filters.
  • Library Scans: what Library Scans found in each photo, including objects and scenes, words recognized in it (such as text in a screenshot), its main colors, the search model's numbers, look-alike data, and camera and lens details (camera make and model, lens model, and actual and 35mm-equivalent focal length). Entries are filed under Apple Photos' cross-device identifier for each photo, with details that tell your devices whether the photo has changed. They never include photos, videos or location coordinates. When a photo is deleted from your library or moved to the Vault, its entry is removed from iCloud with the next backup.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: on iPad and Mac, the keys set in More → Keyboard Shortcuts.

iCloud Sync does not include your photos or videos, Organize progress, SortGlass's Trash or the free allowance. Library Scans results and camera and lens details are stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields, which your device encrypts with keys from your iCloud Keychain. Settings, statistics and saved filters are stored as files in your private iCloud database, which Apple protects according to your iCloud settings (Standard Data Protection or Advanced Data Protection).

Turning a category off stops that device from sending or receiving it; records already saved in iCloud stay there, and a request already in flight may finish. Delete Everything from iCloud, in iCloud Sync, removes SortGlass's settings, backups and scan results from iCloud and turns iCloud Sync off on that device; what's on the device stays. Delete Objects & Text Data also removes Library Scans' objects, scenes and words from iCloud. Your other devices with iCloud Sync on can send theirs again, so turn it off there too. You can also delete SortGlass's data in your Apple Account's iCloud storage settings (Manage Storage), where SortGlass is listed; that removes all of SortGlass's iCloud records, including the Vault's encrypted copy. Reinstalling does not itself remove iCloud records.

The Vault keeps an encrypted copy of its items, their previews and its folders in your private iCloud, so your other devices can open it with the same password. Everything is encrypted on your device before it is sent, with keys protected by your Vault password or recovery key, so iCloud stores only data it cannot read. Neither your password nor your recovery key is sent in readable form. Erase Vault removes the Vault from iCloud and from your other devices.

Separately, Apple may synchronize your Photos library through iCloud Photos or include app data in device backups. Those services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's practices. SortGlass keeps Library Scans results and the Vault out of device backups. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete copies in backups. This policy does not promise storage in a particular country or universal end-to-end encryption.

5. Photo locations and Apple services

When you view a photo's details or a map, or use Trips & Events, Apple's mapping and geocoding services may process the coordinates recorded in a photo to show a map or a place name. To make places searchable, SortGlass groups photos by where they were taken, to about a kilometer, and asks Apple Maps for each area's names, sending only that rounded position, never a photo. When you type a place, such as a home for Trips or a new place in Adjust Date & Place, Apple Maps looks it up; for a home, only its area is kept. These are your photos' recorded locations and places you type, not your device's current location; SortGlass does not ask for location access.

Apple also provides platform services used by SortGlass. Learn about Apple's handling of information in the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions. SortGlass uses Apple's purchase information, such as product identifiers, transaction information, and entitlement status, to verify access and restore purchases. SortGlass does not receive your full payment-card information.

Apple may provide sales and financial reports for operating the app business. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account. Deleting the app or clearing statistics does not cancel a subscription.

Optional tips are one-time in-app purchases processed by Apple. They unlock nothing, and we receive only Apple's standard sales records for them. Offer codes are redeemed in Apple's own code sheet or the App Store.

7. TestFlight and diagnostics

When you use a TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects and shares crash logs and beta usage information with the developer. This can include installation and session information, device and operating-system details, and app versions. Your name and email may be available for email invitations; Apple does not display them to the developer merely because you join through a public link.

Feedback you submit can include comments, screenshots, and technical information. Avoid including private photos or other sensitive content unnecessarily. We use beta information to investigate problems and improve SortGlass, and do not share TestFlight information with third parties. TestFlight's automatic collection cannot be opted out of while participating. See TestFlight & Privacy.

For public releases, Apple may also provide crash, diagnostic, sales, and usage reports under Apple's services and applicable settings. Receiving these reports does not give us access to your private Photos library.

8. Sharing and notifications

You choose whether to share, copy or drag media into another app, share progress, or send feedback. The destination you select may receive the shared content and associated metadata under its own privacy practices. Copying content places it on the system clipboard. Export as JPEG lets you leave out the date and location. Check content and recipients before sharing.

With your permission, SortGlass schedules local notifications: On This Day (photos taken on today's date in earlier years), Photos to Sort and Streaks reminders, at most one of these a day; Cleanup reminders on the schedule you choose, when Cleanup has something new; a Monthly Recap; reminders for goals you turn them on for; and a notice when Library Scans finishes while you're away. SortGlass works them out on your device from your photos' dates, what is waiting to be sorted or cleaned up, your progress and the days you used the app, and your device shows them; no notification content is sent to us or to any server. Each has its own switch in More → Notifications or in its goal, and you can change notification permission in your device's Settings.

SortGlass's Home Screen widgets show counts and, for On This Day, a photo from that day. While Library Scans runs in the background, the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island and the Apple Watch Smart Stack show only how far it has got, never photos or the words found in them.

SortGlass may show Apple's standard rating prompt; Apple handles ratings and reviews. Send Feedback and Email Support open your email app. Give Back's links and Donate buttons open each organization's own website in your browser; SortGlass does not see those pages, any payment, or whether you gave.

9. Support email

If you contact support@sortglass.com, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and associated email information. We use this information to answer questions, troubleshoot issues, handle privacy requests, and maintain necessary support records.

Our email service is Spacemail by Spaceship, which processes and stores correspondence to deliver the service. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary private photos. See Spaceship's Privacy Policy.

10. Website hosting

sortglass.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives technical request information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the website. Its handling and retention are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

This privacy-policy page does not embed analytics scripts, advertisements, contact forms, or tracking pixels. Following links to other websites subjects your visit to their practices. Email links open your chosen email application; information is sent when you send a message.

The homepage loads App Store badge images from Apple's marketing tools service. This makes a request to Apple, which receives technical information such as your IP address and browser request details to deliver the image. The homepage's interactive demonstrations run in your browser and do not access your Photos library.

11. Purposes and legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we process information as necessary to provide the features and support you request, based on consent for optional processing where applicable, to meet legal obligations, and for legitimate interests in maintaining security, resolving technical problems, and improving reliability where those interests are not overridden by your rights. A device permission is an access control and does not by itself establish the legal basis for every processing activity.

When processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it through the relevant controls or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Apple and other providers may determine their own legal bases for information they process independently.

12. Disclosure and international processing

Information is handled by the providers identified above for the described services. We may disclose operator-held information when legally required, to protect rights and security as permitted by law, or at your direction. The restrictions on TestFlight information described above continue to apply.

We operate in the United States. Using our website, sending support email, or using Apple services can involve processing outside your country, where privacy laws may differ. Providers describe their processing and transfer arrangements in their linked notices. Contact us for information about arrangements applicable to operator-held information. This policy does not claim that all data remains in your country or that the operator is certified under an international transfer framework.

In a lawful business transfer, operator-held information may transfer subject to applicable law, provider restrictions, and the privacy commitments under which it was obtained. A change of ownership does not automatically grant access to your private Photos library or authorize incompatible new uses. We will provide notice and obtain consent where required.

13. Retention and deletion

Local app information remains until removed through available app controls, deletion of app data, or system storage management. Offloading an app may retain its data. Apple's backups and Photos library have separate retention and deletion controls.

We retain support correspondence and operator-held diagnostic or beta records only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve requests, investigate related problems, maintain security, or meet legal obligations. When no longer needed for those purposes, we delete them. Relevant factors include whether an issue is unresolved, whether a recurring defect needs investigation, and whether a legal retention requirement applies. You may request earlier deletion, subject to applicable exceptions.

Apple, GitHub, and Spaceship manage information in their services under their own retention practices. Deleting an operator-held copy does not necessarily remove a provider's independent records. We cannot directly erase your private Photos library or Apple device backups for you.

14. Your choices and privacy rights

You can adjust Photos and notification permissions, turn iCloud Sync and each of its categories on or off, delete SortGlass's data from iCloud, delete what Library Scans found, clear dashboard statistics, erase the Vault, manage Apple backups and subscriptions, stop beta participation, and choose whether to share content or contact support.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a portable copy of personal information; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; and complain to a privacy regulator. Where applicable, you may appeal a decision by replying to our response and requesting review. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

Send requests to support@sortglass.com. We may request information reasonably needed to verify your identity or authority, but will not ask for your entire photo library. Rights and exceptions depend on the applicable law and whether the information is held by us or independently by a provider.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

15. Children and security

Photo-library access does not by itself send a child's photos to the developer. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support or beta feedback without any authorization required by applicable law, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

App Lock and the Vault use Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode through iOS. SortGlass learns only whether you were recognized and never receives biometric data. The Vault uses Face ID only if you turn it on. If you forget the Vault password and lose the recovery key, no one, including us, can open the Vault.

SortGlass relies on iOS permissions and platform storage protections for local information. No device, service, or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in email or feedback.

16. Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy as features or information practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before implementing a change.
