Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

SortGlass is designed to help you organize your photo library while minimizing the collection of personal information.

Photo Library Access

SortGlass may request access to your photo library so that you can view, organize, rate, favorite, add photos or videos to albums, and select items for deletion.

Your photos and videos are processed on your device. SortGlass does not upload your photo library to SortGlass-operated servers.

Information We Collect

SortGlass does not sell your personal information.

The app may store information necessary to provide its features, such as app preferences, organization data, ratings, and usage statistics.

iCloud

If you enable features that use iCloud, certain SortGlass app data may be stored in your personal iCloud account and synchronized across your devices using Apple's services.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. SortGlass does not receive your full payment-card information.

Third-Party Services

SortGlass may rely on Apple services that are necessary for app functionality, such as the App Store, Photos framework, and iCloud.

Data Sharing

SortGlass does not sell your personal information or your photo-library content to advertisers or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

SortGlass is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SortGlass changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to support@sortglass.com.
