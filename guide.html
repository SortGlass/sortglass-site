Using SortGlass

Getting started

SortGlass works on the Apple Photos library already on your iPhone. There’s no account to create and nothing to import.

  1. Download SortGlass from the App Store. It requires iOS 18 or later.
  2. When asked, allow access to your photos. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SortGlass. Restricting access limits what SortGlass can show and organize.
  3. Choose what to organize in Browse, or go straight to Organize to start with everything.

Connected to Apple Photos. Favorites, album changes, ratings on iOS 27 or later, and confirmed deletions apply to your Photos library too. Sending an item to SortGlass Trash only queues it: you review and confirm before it’s deleted from Photos.

The five tabs

  • Library: see the whole library, select in bulk, and revisit your rated photos.
  • Browse: choose the photos and videos you want to organize.
  • Organize: review one at a time. Favorite, rate, add to albums, or send to Trash.
  • Albums: explore and manage your albums and folders.
  • More: your settings, appearance, progress, and this guide.

Organize

A little focus. A lot of progress. Organize shows one photo or video at a time, with your albums beneath it.

Swipe to keep moving

Swipe sideways to browse, down to favorite, and up to send a photo or video to Trash.

Give photos a home

Tap an album beneath the photo to add it. A colored album icon means it’s already there; tap again to remove it without advancing. New creates an album or folder. A photo can belong to several albums.

Choose your strongest shots

With Pro on iOS 27 or later, tap Set Rating, then a star. Ratings save directly to your Apple Photos library, not just SortGlass. Revisit them in Library or filter them in Browse for a second pass.

Undo, redo, and inspect

The curved arrows undo and redo recent actions. History lets you revisit and undo your last 100 album and Trash actions. Tap the information button for photo details.

Browse

Start with what you want to organize. A smaller, focused set is a session you can finish.

All or Unsorted

Start with everything, or choose Unsorted for items that are not in an album.

Jump to a year

The year tiles show how many matching items were taken that year. Tap a year to start there in Organize.

Focus with Pro

Combine filters for favorites, media and file types, albums, dates, and ratings on supported devices. With Pro, you can also customize your filters and save named combinations to use again.

Two ways in

Open the results in Library for a grid, or in Organize to review them one at a time.

Library

The bigger picture, all in one place.

See your full library

Browse your photos and videos. Tap an item for a closer look.

Work with several at once

Select multiple items to send to Trash, add to an album, or favorite.

Choose your grid

Pinch to change the number of photos per row, from 3 to 8, and use the view options to adjust the layout.

Revisit your ratings

See rated photos again and compare your picks. Ratings are available on iOS 27 or later.

Albums

Everything in its place.

Browse and manage

Search for an album or folder, then open it to browse. Choose Organize Album when you want to review its contents one at a time.

Keep favorites close

Pin frequently used albums to put them in Organize’s album row. Use the Pinned view to find them quickly.

Arrange your workflow

Reorder your pinned albums to put the ones you reach for most first.

Trash and deleting

Swiping up queues an item in SortGlass Trash. Nothing is deleted from your library at that point.

  1. Open Trash to review the queue. You can take items back out.
  2. Choose Delete. iOS asks you to confirm.
  3. Confirmed items move to Recently Deleted in Apple Photos, where Apple generally keeps them for up to 30 days unless you delete them sooner.

To recover something after deleting it, open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted and choose Recover.

Free and Pro

SortGlass is free to download. The free version includes up to 100 Trash and Album actions each week; Favorites, Browse, and Undo are always included. The weekly count starts again every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

SortGlass Pro unlocks unlimited organizing, star ratings (iOS 27 or later), combined and saved filters, and every theme, app icon and accent color. Every Pro feature is included with any Pro subscription or the Lifetime purchase.

Restore a purchase

Open More → SortGlass Pro → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Cancel a subscription

Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription, and buying Lifetime doesn’t cancel an existing one.

Redeem an offer code

In the App Store app, tap your account picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.
